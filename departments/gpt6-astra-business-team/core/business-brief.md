# Business Brief Template

**Complete this once, then maintain it as the shared source of context. Replace bracketed fields with facts. Keep unknown items explicit and attach evidence for claims or numbers. Share only information the task needs.**

---

## Fields

| # | Field | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | **Business and website** | [name and URL] |
| 2 | **What we sell** | [offer, deliverables, price, currency and billing basis] |
| 3 | **Who we serve** | [customer type, buyer, geography and exclusions] |
| 4 | **Customer problem** | [job, current alternative and desired outcome] |
| 5 | **Why buyers choose us** | [specific differences with supporting evidence] |
| 6 | **Current objective** | [one measurable outcome and date] |
| 7 | **Baseline** | [current result, period, definition and source] |
| 8 | **Capacity** | [people, available hours and delivery limits] |
| 9 | **Budget** | [approved ceiling and spending authority] |
| 10 | **Brand voice** | [three examples and language to avoid] |
| 11 | **Approved claims** | [claim, evidence, scope and permission] |
| 12 | **Systems and data** | [available tools, exports and source of truth] |
| 13 | **Permissions** | [read, draft and write actions actually authorised] |
| 14 | **Financial context** | [entity, jurisdiction, currency and accounting basis] |
| 15 | **Working conventions** | [timezone, date format and language] |
| 16 | **Decision owner** | [person responsible for the final decision] |
| 17 | **Material constraints** | [privacy, commercial or operational limits] |
| 18 | **Brief version and date** | [version, author and date] |

---

## Usage

1. Fill all 18 fields with current, evidence-backed information
2. Share the completed brief with every role prompt
3. Update when assumptions change — name affected tasks and ask those roles to revise
4. Version every change (v1.0, v1.1, etc.) with date and author

---

## A Useful Objective

**Weak:** improve marketing  
**Better:** prepare a four-week campaign for our customer onboarding service, using a GBP 1,500 budget and no more than six hours of production time per week. The goal is to learn which of two audience problems produces more qualified enquiries. The current baseline and qualification definition are attached.

---

## Resolve Conflicting Information

Specify which source owns each fact:
- Signed agreement → commercial terms
- Reviewed ledger → accounting actuals
- Approved campaign brief → current message

Ask the owner when equally authoritative sources disagree. Do not silently choose the more convenient number.
