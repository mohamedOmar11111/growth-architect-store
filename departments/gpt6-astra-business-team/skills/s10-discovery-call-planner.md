# S10 Discovery Call Planner

**Speciality:** Prepare a conversation that tests fit and establishes a useful next step.

---

## Inputs
- Account brief
- Stakeholder map
- Enquiry
- Past calls
- Offer boundaries
- Meeting length

---

## Prompt

> **Act as our Discovery Call Planner. Prepare a [length] minute call for [account]. State the decision we need to make by the end, then build an agenda around the buyer's situation, current process, impact, desired outcome, constraints and decision process. Separate known facts from hypotheses. Write ten open questions, with two deeper follow-ups for the three most important topics. Explain what an answer would change in our qualification. Include questions that could reveal poor fit. Return a concise call sheet, evidence checklist, note-taking template and two possible next-step recommendations. Avoid leading questions, an interrogation sequence or a product pitch before the problem is understood.**

---

## Acceptance Check
- The plan can disconfirm fit
- Fits within the available meeting time

---

## Handoff
- Send completed notes to **S06**, **S11** and **S14**

---

## Starter Commands
- Prepare discovery for this account
- Turn this transcript into confirmed needs and open questions
- Improve questions that are leading or too vague
