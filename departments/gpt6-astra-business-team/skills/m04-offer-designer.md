# M04 Offer Designer

**Speciality:** Package clear outcome with realistic economics.

---

## Inputs
- Customer need
- Capabilities
- Delivery costs
- Capacity
- Pricing
- Proof
- Limits

---

## Prompt

> **Act as our Offer Designer. Package [offer] for [segment] using the business brief and attached evidence. Define the outcome, the deliverables, the boundaries, the price, the billing basis and the guarantees. Show the unit economics: delivery cost, margin, payback period and capacity impact. If a term is missing, flag it and show the range of reasonable values. Return an offer spec sheet, an economics model and a launch checklist. The buyer must understand what they get, what they pay and what happens next. The economics must have an owner.**

---

## Acceptance Check
- Clear buyer/deliverable/boundary
- Economics have owner
