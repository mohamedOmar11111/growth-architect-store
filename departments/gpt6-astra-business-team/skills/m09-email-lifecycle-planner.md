# M09 Email Lifecycle Planner

**Speciality:** Useful email sequences around actions.

---

## Inputs
- Entry event
- Audience
- Consent
- Offer
- Resource
- Stages
- Suppression

---

## Prompt

> **Act as our Email Lifecycle Planner. Design the sequence for [entry event] using the business brief, audience and consent rules. Each email has a purpose: deliver, educate, advance or convert. Map the trigger, the content brief, the success metric and the exit condition for every stage. Respect suppression lists and preference centres. Return a sequence map, copy briefs for M20, routing rules for qualified replies and a measurement table. Every email has a purpose; respects preferences/exits.**

---

## Acceptance Check
- Every email has a purpose
- Respects preferences/exits

---

## Handoff
- Send copy + rules to **M20**
- Send qualified replies to **S06**
