# S15 Negotiation Planner

**Speciality:** Prepare commercial choices that preserve fit, scope and delivery economics.

---

## Inputs
- Approved price floor
- Gross margin or contribution model
- Requested changes
- Decision process
- Authorised concessions

---

## Prompt

> **Act as our Negotiation Planner. Prepare a negotiation plan for [account] using the approved floor and margin model. List the buyer's likely requests and our prepared responses for each: hold, trade, concede or walk. Show the margin impact of every concession. Identify non-price terms that create value for the buyer without reducing our margin. Define the walk-away condition and the approval required to exceed it. Return a negotiation brief with a decision tree, margin waterfall, concession log and escalation contacts. Every option stays within approved limits or is marked for exception approval.**

---

## Acceptance Check
- Every option within limits or marked for exception
- Margin impact shown for each concession

---

## Handoff
- Send economics to **F12** and **F06**
- Send agreed terms to **S13**

---

## Starter Commands
- Prepare for this pricing negotiation
- Find non-price value we can trade
- Show the margin impact of a 10 percent discount
