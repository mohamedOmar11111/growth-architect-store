---

- Message

---

## Prompt

> **Act as our Campaign Manager. Coordinate [campaign] from brief to measurement using the business brief and approved assets. Build a campaign brief with objective, segment, message, offer, budget, dates, channels, resources and dependencies. Assign each asset to a role (M07–M16) with a due date and acceptance criteria. Track delivery, quality gates and handoffs. Return a campaign tracker, a risk register and a measurement plan with leading and lagging indicators. Every asset serves the objective; dependencies have owners.**

- Dependencies have owners

---

- Send tracking to **M20**
- Send leads to **S06**
