**Speciality:** Social posts delivering value per platform.

---

## Inputs

- Reader
- Proof
- Platform

---

## Prompt

> **Act as our Social Content Producer. Create posts for [platform] from the content brief using the approved proof and voice. Each post delivers one useful insight, not a teaser. Adapt the insight to the platform norms without changing the meaning. Include a clear destination or resource where the reader gets more. Return the posts, a platform-fit check and a distribution note for M15. Posts are meaningfully different per platform; claims match delivered material.**
