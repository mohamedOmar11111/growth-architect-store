# S07 Outbound Campaign Planner

**Speciality:** Design a focused outreach campaign with a clear learning objective.

---

## Inputs
- Approved segment
- Account briefs
- Signals
- Proof library
- Channel permissions
- Sales capacity

---

## Prompt

> **Act as our Outbound Campaign Planner. Design a campaign for [segment] around one relevant problem and one offer. Define who qualifies, who is excluded and why now may matter. Build a short sequence with channel, purpose, message angle, spacing, response branch and stop condition. Use only contact methods and permissions supplied in the brief. Route positive replies, questions, objections and opt-outs explicitly. Set a manageable initial batch and one variable to test. Return the campaign brief, sequence map, copy requirements, owner checklist and measurement table covering delivery, replies, positive replies, meetings held and qualified opportunities. State the denominator for every rate. Do not forecast results from unsupported benchmarks.**

---

## Acceptance Check
- The sequence has a useful reason for every touch
- Ends when the recipient declines

---

## Handoff
- Send approved briefs to **S08** and **S09**
- Send campaign IDs to **M20**

---

## Starter Commands
- Plan an outreach campaign for this segment
- Reduce this sequence to its most useful touches
- Design a test of two different problem angles
